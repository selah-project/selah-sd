# ترجمي جا نوٽ — Translation Notes

*هي فائيل اهي آيتون ڏيکاري ٿي جتي انساني هٿ يا ڪو فيصلو ترجمي کي ڇهيو. باقي نوٽ انگريزيءَ ۾ آهن — اهي عبراني گرامر ۽ حوالن جو ذڪر ڪن ٿا.*

This file records every verse where a human hand or a ruling
touched the Sindhi rendering — so any verse can be audited: was it
machine-pressed under the rails, hand-rendered, or ruled, and why.
Rails: the Selah Sindhi discipline (ياهوه / ايلوهيم at the Name
seat; ⟨את⟩ total; ⟨…⟩ marks supplied words only).

## Hand-rendered / hand-spliced verses (2026-08-30)

- **Ruth 2:9** — a machine artifact ("fieldset") healed into
  انهيءَ ٻنيءَ (the field, בשדה). The verse is Boaz's charge over
  Ruth; it resisted three automated passes.
- **Psalm 148:6** — the token glosses were written by hand against
  the Hebrew's seven words (ויעמידם לעד לעולם חק נתן ולא יעבור)
  after the machine returned an empty token line.
- **Deuteronomy 32:52** — the sentence was hand-rendered from the
  Hebrew tokens after repeated garbled output; ⟨the⟩ fills dropped
  (Sindhi has no article).
- **Amos 4:1** — opening clause hand-rendered; script-bleed
  removed; בשן rendered بشن, the needy محتاجن.
- **Haggai 1:1** — a mixed-script splice healed: يهوداه (Judah).
- **Psalm 18:9** — an invisible bidi control character removed.
- **Numbers 32:33** — ⟨the⟩ fill dropped.

## Per-token review verdicts — the خدا / الله floor (2026-08-30)

The generic god-word خدا and الله were reviewed verse by verse
against the Hebrew seat. KEPT as lawful (common elohim — idols,
foreign gods, mocked gods): Jeremiah 16:20 · Exodus 34:17 ·
Daniel 11:8 · 2 Chronicles 13:9 · 2 Kings 19:18 · 1 Kings 18:25,
27. HEALED to the rails' forms (the true-God seat):

- **Genesis 16:13** — El Roi's parenthetical: (ڏسندڙ جو ايل) —
  the name is EL Roi.
- **Exodus 4:27** — הר האלהים: ايلوهيم جي جبل (the mountain of
  Elohim).
- **2 Kings 8:7, 8:8** — איש האלהים: ايلوهيم جو ماڻهو (the man of
  Elohim, Elisha).
- **2 Chronicles 30:19** — האלהים beside YHWH: ايلوهيم ياهوه.
- **Daniel 7:25** — Aramaic עליא (the Most High): the supplied
  god-word fill was removed — مٿاهين needs no supplement.
